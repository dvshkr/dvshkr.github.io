About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at IIT Mandi interested in machine learning, computer vision, scientific computing, and physics-driven research. My work has largely focused on applying computational methods to problems in astronomy and observational science.

I have worked at the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), Ahmedabad, on deep learning methods for solar radio burst classification, involving signal processing and analysis of large observational datasets. At the Indian Astronomical Observatory, Hanle, I worked on automated telescope software, all-sky imaging pipelines, and atmospheric seeing measurement systems.

More recently, I have also explored multimodal learning, 3D computer vision, and neural rendering, including research on language-guided Gaussian Splatting during my exchange semester at the Technical University of Munich (TUM). Broadly, I am interested in building computational tools and learning systems that are useful for scientific discovery and understanding complex physical systems.
